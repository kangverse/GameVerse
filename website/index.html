01 OVERVIEW

A longer view
of interactive worlds.

World models need more than glimpses. GameVerse preserves continuous gameplay sequences up to two minutes, pairing visual dynamics with camera estimates and semantic descriptions across first- and third-person perspectives.

Its construction pipeline combines scene-consistent segmentation, video filtering, camera estimation, and structured captioning. The dataset is designed to support long-horizon video generation, camera-aware understanding, and interactive world modeling.
